## Finding More Competitors

When searching for competitors in a specific bracket:

1. Go to `games-stats.com/steam/?tag=<tag>` and filter by release date (last 3 years)
2. Search: `site:vginsights.com <genre> roguelite revenue 2024 2025`
3. Search: `<genre> roguelite steam "overwhelmingly positive" 2023 OR 2024 OR 2025 revenue`
4. Check howtomarketagame.com annual roundup for the most recent year

Always note: revenue estimates on Steam are estimates (typically using the "Boxleiter method" or review-based multipliers). They are approximate but directionally correct.
